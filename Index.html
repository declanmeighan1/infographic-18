⚠ Key Developments — Days 26–27
▲ CRITICAL — NEW

Iran Rejects US 15-Point Ceasefire; Issues Counteroffer

Iran formally rejected the US ceasefire proposal via Pakistani intermediaries on 25 Mar. Iran’s five-point counteroffer demands sovereign control over the Strait of Hormuz, reparations for war damage, halt to assassinations of officials, and permanent halt to hostilities. Hormuz sovereignty demand assessed as a US non-starter — no ceasefire likely before ~28 Mar moratorium expiry.

▲ UPDATED

Trump 5-Day Diplomatic Window — Expires ~28 Mar

Trump postponed the Hormuz power-plant ultimatum on D+23 for 5 days, citing “productive talks” with a “top person” in Iran via Kushner/Witkoff. Characterised Iran as “eager to deal.” If the window expires without Hormuz reopening, the power-plant strike threat reactivates. Binary inflection point: talks gain traction or US airstrikes on Iranian energy infrastructure resume.

▲ UPDATED

Interceptor Attrition — Strain After 27 Days

UAE air defences have tracked 599 BMs, 2,474 drones, 15 CMs — most intercepted but debris causing civilian casualties. Analysts note air defence capabilities are increasingly strained after nearly four weeks of continuous intercepts. Greek-operated Patriot systems intercepting missiles targeting Saudi oil refineries. UK RAF Typhoon and F-35 jets conducting defensive patrols over Jordan, Qatar, UAE and Bahrain.

▲ UPDATED

Kuwait Airport Fuel Depot Struck by Drone

25 Mar: Iranian drone struck fuel tank at Kuwait International Airport, sparking fire — material damage, no casualties. IRGC claimed 4 missile launches at Camp Arifjan in same strike package. Cumulative: 67+ army WIA; Camp Canada at Ali Al Salem AB hit; Mina Al-Ahmadi refinery struck multiple times; 6 power lines disrupted. Airport strike represents deliberate civilian infrastructure targeting.

▲ UPDATED

Israel — Lebanon Occupation & Continued Barrages

Israel announced intent to occupy southern Lebanon to Litani River as “security zone” — potentially 10% of Lebanese territory. Iranian missile struck Tel Aviv on 24 Mar (100kg warhead, 4 light injuries). 8 salvos on 24 Mar alone. 18+ killed in Israel since war start. Lebanon banned Hezbollah military activities and expelled Iranian ambassador 24 Mar. 1,072 killed, 1.2M displaced in Lebanon.

▲ UPDATED

UAE — Retaliatory Action Under Consideration

UAE MoFA stated country “reserves full right to self-defence” and is considering retaliatory action against Iranian missile sites — unprecedented posture reflecting escalating anger. Cumulative: 11 KIA (2 military, 9 civilian foreign nationals), 166 WIA. Ruwais refinery 922k bpd offline, Jebel Ali Port fire, AWS data centre struck. UAE remains most heavily targeted GCC state (~48% of all Iranian projectiles).

Short-Term Threat Outlook

Day 27 is defined by the diplomatic-military dual track: Iran’s formal rejection of the US 15-point ceasefire and its counter-demand for Hormuz sovereignty eliminate the prospect of a ceasefire before Trump’s ~28 Mar power-plant strike moratorium expires. The approaching deadline creates a binary pressure point — either talks gain traction or US airstrikes on Iranian energy infrastructure resume, likely triggering a new Iranian offensive wave against GCC energy targets. Air defence strain is acute after 27 days of continuous intercepts. Iran’s attack rate is reportedly down ~90% from Day 1, but cost asymmetry ($2–5M per 100 drones vs $200–410M in interceptors) still favours Iran. Expiry of the 5-day moratorium on ~28 Mar is the single most significant near-term escalation trigger.
